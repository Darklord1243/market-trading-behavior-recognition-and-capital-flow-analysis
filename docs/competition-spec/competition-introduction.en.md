## Competition rules

### 1. Eligibility

Open globally to individuals from universities, research institutions, and enterprises via the Tianchi platform. Participants may register for **one category only** (Challenge Group **or** Startup Group). Challenge Group participants may select **multiple tracks**.

### 2. Team formation

- Compete individually or in a team of **up to 3 members**
- Each participant may join **only one team**
- Within the Challenge Group, one team may enter **multiple tracks**

### 3. Identity verification

All participants must complete **real-name authentication**. Unverified participants cannot enter **B-board evaluation** or the **final roadshow**.

### 4. Information requirements

Teams must provide all requested information and supplement it when required by the organizing committee.

### 5. Restrictions

- Organizing committee members, expert judges, their research teams, and anyone with insider access to competition background material are **disqualified**
- Ant Group employees (including subsidiaries) may compete on the leaderboard but are **ineligible for awards and prize money**

Four stages: **Registration & Authentication → Code Submission → Report Submission → Final Roadshow**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Timeline** | June 8, 2026 (Mon) 10:00 — July 20, 2026 (Mon) 12:00 |
| **Registration** | Log in to the track page and complete personal information |
| **Team rules** | Individual or team (max 3); one team per person; multiple tracks allowed |

| **Real-name auth** | **All members** must complete authentication by **July 20, 2026, 12:00**. Entry: Tianchi User Center → Authentication → Alipay, or [tianchi.aliyun.com/home/identification](https://tianchi.aliyun.com/home/identification). Unverified teams are disqualified |

| | |
|---|---|

| | |
