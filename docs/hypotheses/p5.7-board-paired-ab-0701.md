| Method | Upload time | Instant score | Task-1 offline (prod-label DTW-sil) |

**Δ total = −0.0192** on the **same data day**.

This is **not** cross-day “hard-key variance.” Cross-day swings (0.33↔0.52 with zero code changes) compare **different days / different keys**. This comparison isolates **Task-1 labels only**.

**Scoring determinism confirmed (2026-07-04 23:17):** re-upload of the **identical** euclidean 0701 zip scored **0.5245** again (same as 2026-07-02 23:50). Therefore **−0.0192 is a hard measurement**, not noise or judge variance. At ~40% Task-1 weight, that implies a Task-1 sub-channel drop of roughly **−0.048** (0.0192 / 0.40) — non-trivial.

**Directional conclusion:** the one controlled board test we have says labels that **win offline DTW-sil** can **lose on the board’s Task-1 metric**. First evidence that **board Task-1 ≠ our DTW-silhouette proxy** (interpretability judging, Wasserstein blend, or other unknown weighting may dominate).

**Decision (standing through A-board unless evidence updates):**

- **Exploit on scored A-board days:** submit with **`TASK1_METHOD=euclidean`** (git default; **board-favored on only controlled test**, determinism-confirmed).
- **Do not revert P5.7** — machinery stays; path remains available for deliberate A/B.
- **Explore dtw-complete only on explicit human direction** (low-stakes slot, or post–A-board qualification) — not as the forward default on scored days.
- **P0 unchanged:** never miss a daily submit; missed day = 0 on moving average (dominates ±0.02 method choice).

Fable 5’s “+0 to ~+0.15/day board upside” prior for P5.7 should be **lowered** after this datapoint (−0.02 on first controlled test), not dismissed.
